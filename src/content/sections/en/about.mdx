#### Who We are

St. Vladimir Memorial Church is a Russian Orthodox Church in the jurisdiction of the Russian Orthodox Church Outside
of Russia ([ROCOR](https://www.synod.com)).

Our magnificent church was built in commemoration of the millennium of the Baptism of Rus' by Prince Vladimir.

#### Church Life

Every Sunday we serve Divine Liturgy.

Sisterhood makes luncheons every Sunday, following the Divine Liturgy.

We celebrate the twelve Great Feasts.

Special feasts during the year:

* **St. Vladimir's Day** — the last Sunday of July.
  Our patronal feast: Divine Liturgy and the Blessing of Water, followed by a festival
  with Russian music and food and a book and icon stall.
* **Bliny Feast** — a Sunday early in the pre-Lenten season.
  Blini, fish, caviar, raffles, and folk music. Join us and help raise funds for
  St. Vladimir Memorial Church — everyone is welcome!
